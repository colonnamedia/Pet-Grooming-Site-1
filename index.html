First-visit guidance

A little preparation makes the first visit smoother.

These instructions are samples. The grooming business must approve its own intake, health, vaccination, handling, matting, and pickup rules.

Preview pet intake
  1. 01

    Share coat and handling details

    Describe matting, sensitivities, recent procedures, bite history, mobility needs, and anything that helps staff plan safely.

  2. 02

    Confirm document requirements

    Real vaccination or veterinary document collection stays disabled until the client chooses a secure provider and approves written policies.

  3. 03

    Wait for confirmation

    A request is not an appointment. The business confirms service fit, timing, provider, price range, and arrival instructions.
